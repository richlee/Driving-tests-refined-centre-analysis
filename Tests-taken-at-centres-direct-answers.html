1. Counts by day, week and month

Monthly counts and volume rankings: yes, directly available. Exact daily/weekly centre counts and current peak weekdays: not yet verified from an accessible complete dataset. The previous report gave too much prominence to waiting-time proxies. For your question, the primary measure is tests conducted.

Terminology: these are tests taken with an outcome. If “bookings” means appointments created on a particular day, that is a different event and requires booking-creation dates. Neither actual tests nor the future-booking stock supplies those dates.

Scope: DRT122A’s notes include B and B1. Treat these figures as published car-test volumes; a strict B-only extract requires DVSA confirmation. Suppressed values remain missing. No daily or weekly estimates have been made by dividing monthly or annual totals.

What is known about peak days?

An official historical DSA response gives GB FY2010–11 counts: Tuesday 326,436; Wednesday 325,923; Thursday 322,965; Monday 276,547; Friday 266,198; Saturday 82,699; Sunday 4,828. Tuesday was highest in that specific old national period. It cannot be used as evidence of the current busiest weekday at an English or Welsh centre. Official FOI IA/01205/11, Annex A.
